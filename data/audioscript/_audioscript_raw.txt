PK  Yes, of course the lion is dangerous.
You mustn’t take the meat away from

during the day.
Interviewer  Why’s that?
PK  During the day it’s very hot. And the
big cats can’t run fast when it’s hot. A
man can run even when it’s very hot,
but lions can’t.
Interviewer  No?
PK  No, they can’t. When they eat some
meat, they feel very, very hot and get
very tired. So they often just lie around
and they sleep all the time during the
day. At night they are more dangerous
because they don’t sleep. And at night
their eyes are better than your eyes.
So you can’t get the kill from the lion
at night.
Interviewer  OK, so you have to do that

get the kill from the lion?

have to be very quiet. And you go up
to that lion.
Interviewer  Do you attack him with your

surprise him, shock him, so he runs
away.
